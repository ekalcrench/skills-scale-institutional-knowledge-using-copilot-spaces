# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. It covers core product delivery roles as well as specialized supporting roles that contribute to project success.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Collaborate with UX Designers on implementation feasibility

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed
- Design reviews with UX Designers

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics
- Work with Business Analysts to refine requirements
- Align with Customer Support Lead on customer impact

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs
- Customer feedback integration from Support Lead

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication
- Partner with Scrum Master to maintain delivery cadence
- Escalate blockers and dependencies

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation
- Sprint planning and retrospective facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers design user experiences and interfaces that are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with product goals. They collaborate closely with Product Managers, Developers, and Customer Support teams to ensure solutions meet user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate design intent with Developers and Product Managers
- Ensure designs meet accessibility and brand standards
- Incorporate feedback from Customer Support Lead on user pain points
- Document design decisions and rationale

### Goals
- Deliver user-centric, accessible solutions
- Reduce friction in user workflows
- Balance user needs with business and technical constraints

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Developers and Product Managers
- User research findings and insights
- Prototypes and design system documentation
- Customer feedback synthesis from Support Lead

### Interaction Patterns
- **With Developers**: Early feasibility reviews, detailed specifications, and implementation feedback
- **With Product Managers**: Problem validation, user research integration, and prioritization alignment
- **With Customer Support Lead**: Pain point identification, user behavior insights, and beta feedback

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge stakeholders and the delivery team by gathering requirements, refining problem statements, and translating business needs into actionable items for development. They ensure clarity and alignment on scope and acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements from stakeholders
- Refine problem statements and success criteria
- Create and maintain detailed acceptance criteria for backlog items
- Identify dependencies and integration points
- Validate assumptions and clarify ambiguities
- Work with Product Managers on prioritization trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure clear, unambiguous requirements reach the delivery team
- Reduce rework due to misaligned expectations
- Accelerate decision-making through thorough requirement analysis

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and acceptance criteria refinement
- Stakeholder interviews and clarification meetings
- Backlog grooming and prioritization discussions
- Risk and dependency identification in planning sessions

### Interaction Patterns
- **With Product Managers**: Translate vision into detailed requirements
- **With Developers**: Clarify acceptance criteria and edge cases
- **With Stakeholders**: Gather and validate business needs

---

## Site Reliability Engineer (SRE)

### Role Summary
Site Reliability Engineers maintain system reliability, uptime, and observability. They partner with Developers and QA throughout the project lifecycle to ensure deployments are safe, monitored, and recoverable.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain monitoring, alerting, and observability systems
- Plan and execute safe deployments with rollback capabilities
- Develop incident response and mitigation procedures
- Conduct reliability reviews of architectural and deployment decisions
- Partner with Developers on production readiness and performance
- Document runbooks and operational procedures

### Goals
- Maintain high system availability and performance
- Reduce incident impact through fast detection and response
- Enable confident, low-risk deployments

### Typical Communication
- Deployment planning and release coordination
- Reliability reviews and architectural feedback
- Incident postmortems and action item tracking
- Monitoring and alerting dashboards

### Interaction Patterns
- **With Developers**: Pre-deployment readiness, monitoring instrumentation, and incident response
- **With Project Manager**: Release planning and deployment window coordination
- **With QA**: Load testing and failure scenario validation

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove blockers, and coach teams on agile practices and continuous improvement. They support Project Managers and Product Managers in maintaining effective delivery cadence.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help resolve team blockers and impediments
- Coach team members on agile best practices
- Track sprint metrics (velocity, burndown) and team health
- Facilitate retrospectives and action item follow-up
- Support continuous improvement initiatives

### Goals
- Maintain predictable, high-velocity delivery
- Foster psychological safety and team collaboration
- Reduce cycle time through process optimization

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies facilitation
- Blocker escalation and resolution tracking
- Team metrics and retrospective summaries
- Continuous improvement action item tracking

### Interaction Patterns
- **With Project Manager**: Cadence coordination and risk escalation support
- **With Developers**: Blocker resolution and capacity planning
- **With Product Manager**: Backlog readiness and sprint goal alignment

---

## Customer Support Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Support Leads channel customer feedback, issues, and satisfaction signals into the project cycle. They connect product delivery with end-user touchpoints and ensure solutions address real customer pain points.

### Responsibilities
- Collect and synthesize customer feedback and support tickets
- Identify patterns in customer issues and feature requests
- Communicate customer impact and urgency to Product and Project leadership
- Validate solutions with customers during beta or early release phases
- Document customer workflows and pain points
- Provide input on acceptance criteria and success metrics

### Goals
- Ensure product delivery is informed by customer reality
- Reduce customer frustration through targeted improvements
- Accelerate customer adoption and satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and issue trends
- User journey documentation and pain point analysis
- Beta feedback and early access coordination
- Customer satisfaction metrics and NPS tracking

### Interaction Patterns
- **With Product Managers**: Customer feedback prioritization and success metrics alignment
- **With UX Designers**: User pain points and workflow validation
- **With Developers**: Issue reproduction and production incident impact
